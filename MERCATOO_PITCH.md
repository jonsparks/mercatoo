# Mercatoo

## 1. Startup / Product Pitch
**Mercatoo** is the modern, merchant-first ecommerce platform built from the ground up to champion small businesses. If you’re a designer, a dropshipper, a creator of handmade goods, or an Etsy seller looking to finally own your audience, you shouldn't need a computer science degree—or an endless budget—to launch a beautiful online store.

For too long, the industry standard has been bloated, expensive, and overwhelming. Shopify gave us a way to sell online, but it also gave us a maze of complex backend settings, creeping monthly costs, and a steep learning curve. Mercatoo changes the game by being fiercely aggressive on pricing and relentlessly focused on simplicity. We’ve stripped away the backend complexity and kept all the frontend power.

With Mercatoo, you get a highly customizable, drag-and-drop storefront powered by a standardized, intuitive admin panel. You get the tools you actually need—from a built-in CRM and abandoned cart tracking to essential marketing features—right out of the box on day one. By leveraging Supabase as our rock-solid foundation for data and authentication, and Stripe for seamless, secure checkout, we provide enterprise-grade reliability without the enterprise price tag. Mercatoo isn't just a place to host your products; it's a confident, exciting, and practical launchpad for your independence. Build your store on your terms, keep more of your money, and get back to what you do best: making and selling incredible things.

## 6. What Mercatoo Should Explicitly Avoid at Launch
To maintain our focus on simplicity, speed, and reliability, Mercatoo should steer clear of the following during its initial release:
- **A Third-Party App Ecosystem/Store:** Framing Mercatoo around a mature app marketplace at launch dilutes our "everything you need built-in" message. An app ecosystem introduces complexity, security risks, and variable costs for merchants. This is strictly a later-stage expansion.
- **Complex Backend Customization:** We must resist the urge to let users tinker with backend logic, database routing, or raw code injection. The backend must remain standardized to ensure platform stability and a unified user experience.
- **Enterprise B2B Features:** Avoid building complex features like multi-warehouse routing, advanced wholesale tiered pricing, or massive multi-vendor marketplace functionality. We are focusing on small businesses and independent creators first.
- **Proprietary Payment Gateways:** Don't build a custom payment processor or force merchants into a proprietary "Mercatoo Pay" system on day one. Relying entirely on Stripe ensures immediate trust, security, and lower development overhead.
- **Overcomplicated Tax/Compliance Engines:** While basic tax settings are necessary, avoid trying to build a global, real-time tax compliance engine from scratch. Integrate with straightforward, existing solutions or offer simplified settings to start.
